Cookie Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This page explains how Devine Dental Services Limited uses cookies and similar browser storage on this website.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that websites can store on your device to remember information. "Local storage" and "session storage" are similar browser technologies that work in much the same way.

Do We Use Cookies?

This website does not use tracking, analytics, or advertising cookies. We do not track your browsing behaviour across other websites.

The only browser storage this site uses is a small, temporary flag saved in your browser's session storage the first time you visit our homepage, so that the welcome animation doesn't play again if you move between pages during the same visit. This value is cleared automatically when you close the browser tab and is never sent to us or anyone else.

Third-Party Cookies

Some content on this site is loaded from third-party services, which may set their own cookies when their content loads on the page:

  • Google Maps — embedded on our Contact and Home pages to show our clinic location.
  • Google Fonts — used to load the typefaces used across this site.

We don't control these cookies. You can find out more about how Google uses cookies from Google's own privacy and cookie policies.

Managing Cookies

Most web browsers let you control or clear cookies through their settings. Since this site doesn't rely on cookies for its core functionality, you can block or delete cookies without affecting your ability to browse the site, view our services, or contact us.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy if the way this website works changes. Any updates will be posted on this page.
