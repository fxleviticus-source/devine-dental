Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Devine Dental Services Limited ("we", "our", "us") operates this website to share information about our dental clinic in Kitwe, Zambia and to make it easy for patients to get in touch and request an appointment. This page explains what information the site collects and how it's used.

Information You Provide to Us

If you use the appointment request form on our Contact page, you may provide your name, phone number, email address, preferred date, preferred service and a message. This website does not have its own booking database or server — submitting the form opens a pre-filled message for you to send us directly via WhatsApp or email. That information is received and handled by our clinic team the same way any other WhatsApp message or email would be, in order to respond to your appointment request.

If you contact us by phone, WhatsApp, or email directly, we receive whatever information you choose to share with us in that conversation.

Information We Do Not Collect

This website does not use analytics or advertising trackers, does not require you to create an account, and does not store appointment requests on a server or database.

Third-Party Services

A few parts of this website rely on external services, which have their own privacy practices:

  • Google Maps — used on our Contact and Home pages to show our location. Google may set cookies or collect data when the map loads.
  • Google Fonts — used to display the typefaces on this site.
  • WhatsApp (Meta) — used for our WhatsApp contact buttons and the appointment form hand-off. Messages sent this way are subject to WhatsApp's own privacy policy.

We don't control how these third parties handle data — please refer to their own privacy policies for details.

How We Use Your Information

Any information you share with us is used only to respond to your enquiry or appointment request. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Your Rights

You can ask us at any time what information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by contacting us using the details below.

Children's Privacy

This website is intended for general audiences and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through this website.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as our website or services change. Any updates will be posted on this page.
